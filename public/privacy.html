Your design and your privacy.

This configurator preview is operated by Generate Leads Online for review of a proposed Make Room design journey. It is hosted separately from Make Room’s official website.

What we save

When you request an estimate, your first and last name, email, phone number, installation postcode and design are saved. Project timing and budget are saved only if supplied. When you ask for a callback, a show village visit or a brochure, your name, phone number, any email, postcode and notes you add, and your current design are saved. The estimate and PDF are linked to your submitted design. Your contact details do not appear in shared design links or the PDF.

Why we save it

We use these details to provide your estimate, preserve your design and allow the project team to review the request. The preview does not automatically send an email or forward your request into Make Room’s CRM. To speak directly with Make Room, call 01422 580172 or visit their Halifax Show Village page.

Your device and hosting

Your design is saved in your browser’s local storage so you can return on this device. An essential, secure session cookie allows you to reveal revised estimates and download your saved PDF after submitting. The session expires after seven days. Private request and PDF records are stored with Netlify and are accessible to authorised project administrators. No advertising or tracking cookies are set by this preview.

3D diagnostics

If the 3D view cannot run on your device, or has to switch to its simplified mode, your browser sends a short technical report: browser and device type, screen size, graphics chip and the steps the 3D view went through. It contains no name, contact details, design or IP address, and is used only to make the 3D view work on more devices.

Your choices

Marketing permission is optional and unchecked. Submitting a design does not subscribe you to marketing. A marketing preference records your choice; no automated marketing messages are sent by this preview.

For access, correction or deletion of information submitted here, contact Generate Leads Online and include your design reference. Project records should be reviewed and removed when no longer needed; the operator should apply a retention policy before customer acquisition campaigns begin.
