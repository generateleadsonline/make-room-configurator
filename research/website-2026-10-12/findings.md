Read-only. Nothing submitted. 30 real build photos in `photos/` (01–30), swatches in `photos/swatches/`. All image paths are on https://www.makeroomoutside.co.uk.

## What the 3D model must reproduce

- **Roof and fascia:** flat roof on a box, plain anthracite (RAL 7016-like) fascia band ~150–200 mm deep on all sides; black on black builds. A slight fall to the back reads as a wedge from the side. No front gutters.
- **Overhang:** ~300–600 mm at the front. Underside clad in warm cedar-tone boards, even on dark buildings, with recessed LED downlights.
- **Wall lights:** black or stainless up/down cylinder light each side of the main doors.
- **Cladding:** mostly vertical boards. Horizontal lap only on Cedral builds. Slatted/fluted composite in wood-effect or black on newer builds. Slim dark corner trims, not corner boards.
- **Frames:** anthracite by default, black on some. Glazing runs full height up to the soffit.
- **Base:** no visible plinth. Sits low on a grey or dark composite deck or patio; composite skirting hides the steel frame where the ground falls away.
- **Canopies:** posts clad to match the walls, same fascia and soffit treatment.
